## 3. CLEAN SPOKEN NARRATION

Yesterday, we saw how the machine uses a spotlight to filter out the noise. But here is the riddle: How does it know where to point that light if it doesn't even understand the words yet? 

Imagine a chaotic dinner table. Someone shouts the word "Bank!" and the room goes silent. Is it a river bank? A money bank? Immediately, every person at the table shouts a clue. "Fishing!" shouts one. "Interest rates!" shouts another. "Muddy!" yells a third.

Inside Prah-va’s brain, this happens in a heartbeat. Every single word "pokes" every other word simultaneously. They aren’t checking a dictionary; they’re looking for a "vibe match." If "Bank" pokes "River" and feels a spark of resonance, it ignores the "Money" clues and absorbs that "watery" meaning. It’s a massive, parallel high-five where words steal context from their neighbors to find themselves. 

But what happens if one dinner table conversation isn't enough to solve the mystery? What if eight different tables are debating the same sentence from eight different angles? That’s the secret of the "Multi-Head." Find out tomorrow. Follow Prah-va, and keep questioning the machine.
