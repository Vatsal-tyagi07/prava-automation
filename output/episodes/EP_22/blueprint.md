## 3. CLEAN SPOKEN NARRATION

Ever wonder why your brain treats the word "The" like background noise, but "Free" like a siren? Old AI used to treat every word like an equal citizen. It was... a disaster.

Imagine a loud Indian wedding. Uncle ji is yelling about the GDP, the dhol is deafening, and kids are shrieking. Total chaos, right? But the second someone whispers, "Biryani ready hai," the entire room vanishes. Your brain shuts out the noise and beams a spotlight on that one sentence.

In AI, we call this 'Attention.' It uses three things: a Query, a Key, and a Value. The Query is what you’re looking for. The Key is the label on every word in the room. When they match? That’s a 'Dot Product'—a mathematical spark that says: "Ignore the Uncle, focus on the Biryani."

The Value is the actual information you extract. This 'Context Spotlight' is why ChatGPT can understand a 50-page story without forgetting how it started. But what happens when the machine starts paying attention to the wrong things?
